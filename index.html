Why this exists under the surface
Most of what holds a therapist up is below the waterline — and nobody hands you a map to it in graduate school.

Graduate school gave you theory and your first year gave you paperwork, and somewhere in between you were supposed to work out what to do on a Thursday afternoon when a client says something you weren't ready for and there are eleven minutes until the next one. Most clinicians figure that out alone, badly, and at some cost to themselves.

Supervision you can find anywhere. What's harder to find is six or seven people who stay long enough to actually know you — who have heard about this client before, who will push back when your formulation is thin, and who are building the same kind of career you are at roughly the same time.

What actually happens how it works

Six months,
one morning a month.

Live group sessions built around your cases and the person carrying them, with reflective work in between that takes five minutes or two hours depending on the week you're having.

i

Skills & Self-Reflection

We go after the reasoning underneath your interventions. You'll practice saying out loud what you did and why you did it, in front of people who will ask follow-up questions, until your clinical voice sounds like yours rather than a textbook's.

ii

Values, Made Specific

Who you actually want in your caseload, what you'll stop saying yes to, and whether the practice you're building is the one you'd want in ten years or just the one that happened to you. Anchored in systemic thinking and real-time Gestalt processing, while your own training and modalities stay entirely yours.

iii

The Person Doing The Work

Your energy, your attachment patterns, the fears you brought in with you. You are the instrument you work with, so we spend real time getting to know it — in a small, consistent group where honest feedback and a fair amount of laughing both count as the point.

Before you email questions

What people ask first.

I'd like to use the sliding scale — how do I bring that up?

Say so in your first email and that's the whole process. The scale exists because recent graduates exist, and access matters here more than a round number does.

How much work happens between sessions?

The between-session exercises are written to work either as a five-minute skim or a two-hour reflection, and you decide which kind of month you're having. This is meant to add to your life rather than thin it out further.

What if I know I'll have to miss a date?

Plan on missing no more than one session across the six months. The group asks for that because cohesion is most of what makes it work.

Is it monthly or twice a month?

Monthly. It changed in December 2025, after participant feedback and a realistic look at post-graduate budgets.

I want more consultation time than that.

There's an optional consultation group every month on top of the core commitment, at the same rate you're already paying. RSVP whenever you want to come.

Can I join online if I'm traveling?

The monthly Good Company sessions are in person only. The optional consultation sessions run both online and in person, depending on who's coming that month.
