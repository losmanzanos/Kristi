Insurance coverage

What's accepted.

Kaiser Permanente

Kristi has been an affiliated Kaiser Permanente provider since 2024. Coverage and copays depend on your specific plan — worth confirming with Kaiser directly before your first session.

Sondermind

She also sees clients through Sondermind, which works with a number of Colorado insurance plans. Booking through them may be the simplest route depending on your carrier.

Self-pay & out-of-network

Self-pay is welcome. Superbills are provided on request so you can seek out-of-network reimbursement from your insurer.

Worth knowing: using insurance requires a mental health diagnosis on file with your insurer. That is routine and often perfectly fine, but some people prefer to self-pay for privacy. Either choice is reasonable, and Kristi is happy to talk it through.
